I received a couple emails in a row asking if Gingko was "still active", despite putting out what (to me) is quite a lot of updates... I realized that they're all over the place, but not here on the blog.

I'm most definitely still here, working hard and full-time on fixing bugs for current Gingko, and developing the next version (Desktop + web). Most updates for that take place on the [community forums](http://community.gingkoapp.com).

You can follow me on Twitter, as it seems to be the platform I'm most active (though still, not *that* active). <http://twitter.com/gingkoapp>

I also started posting [videos on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCYsafnfyDsVnnAEdivmhk0Q), and will continue to do so, once our schedule returns to normal. For those who aren't aware: my wife and I both work from home, and homeschool our 4.5 yr old son. He's normally with us five days a week, and with the grandparents the other two... except for the last couple months when we've had him 6 or even 7 days a week.

So, that's my mini update. I'm trying to be more visibly active, and I need to get over perfectionism in order to do so... working on that daily!
