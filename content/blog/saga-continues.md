<span style="line-height: 1.5;">We've received almost 3000 submissions to our rebranding contest, and Aleksey and I have just finished going through them.</span>

One thing we learned for sure is that Gingko users are an *extremely* creative group of people! Thank you all for participating.

However, we learned something else.

A few of you asked for more information on *what it is we were after*. And we replied to tell you our vision, the images and themes we had in mind, our own ideas.

And we noticed two things:

1.  The submissions we received afterwards from those people were far closer to what we wanted (duh!).
2.  Those who received that information were more likely to win, meaning the contest was *no longer fair*.

So, I've decided to do two things:

1.  We're extending the contest.
2.  We'll be providing a clearer guideline & vision, which will be kept *open to all* contestants, so that no one has an advantage over anyone else.

We didn't provide any guidelines initially because we felt that we would be stifling creativity.

But I had forgotten that constraints are a great *source* of creativity. And with approximately 200 million possible 6 letter words, some constraints are in order :).

So, if you feel up for the challenge,  
here are the updated guidelines for the contest:

http://gingkoapp.com/rename
