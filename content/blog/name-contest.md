First of all, we wanted to thank everyone who participated in the naming contest. We received far more submissions than we expected, and far more than we were able to sort through easily (close to 3000 submission, each of which had to be looked at and decided on by both of us).

So what's the new name?  
*Well...*

<!--more-->

> "Word of mouth worked for me and I loved the name \[Gingko\]. It landed. At every level before I read your rationale. Even the color is refreshing, freeing. It's fresh and it's stable. It's fun too say. I wouldn't call it a fossil..." - Elizabeth W.

We also realized that the process of changing a name is more complex than we had anticipated. So while we're standing by "Gingko", we did choose our favorite submissions.

After a long process, involving multiple filtered lists, Excel magic, and sorting algorithms, Aleksey and I chose the following winners:

- 1st place: Holly Dugan
- 2nd place: Carl Dean Cox ("*Quality and Quantity!*")
- 3rd place: Michael ("*Most Prolific*")
