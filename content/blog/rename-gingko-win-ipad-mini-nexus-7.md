"Gingko" is looking for a new name.  
  

Why?  

We chose "gingko" in 2012 because the ginkgo tree was associated with growth, branching, and with enhancing memory and concentration.  
  

The name has served us well, but we are striving for something better.  
  

Join our (super simple) naming contest, to win an iPad Mini or Nexus 7, and other prizes, by:  

1.  [Logging in](http://gingkoapp.com/login) (or [signing up](http://gingkoapp.com/signup))
2.  Click on the "Name Contest!" link at the top.
3.  Work your creative muscles, and come up with some names.
4.  Click "Submit Name Ideas" to send us as many as you like.

That's all you need to do to have a chance of winning!  
(contest open to all, till Dec 22)
